- **The C data layer frees what a force of a thunk allocated once the force returns and nothing reaches it: attribute sets, list headers, lambdas, builtins' partial applications and strings with context one by one, and thunk cells, environments and strings a line of 128 bytes at a time, which later allocations reuse.** The layer freed nothing before its session ended, so one evaluation over nixpkgs held every set it had ever made, and sets are more than half of what it allocates. At the end of each force that allocated at least 16 KiB, a collector marks from the stores the force made into older cells and with-scopes and the roots it registered, entering only what no such close inside it already settled, and marks the force's whole region again once the closes inside have tenured 1 MiB since its last such mark; what the mark leaves unreached is freed. A line of a block of cells, environments or strings is freed once every object in it is, and an allocator that has filled its room next takes a run of free lines, numbered as a room of its own so that its objects are younger than every force under way. An evaluation under `NOVA_NIX_C_STATS` or `NOVA_NIX_REGION_STATS` frees nothing, since those censuses count what the evaluation allocated. A force at the top of an evaluation that allocates less than 16 KiB marks nothing, so what it leaves stays until the session ends. (#466)
- **Library API:** `SessionSettings` takes a fifth field, `sessionRegions`, what the session runs regions for (`Nix.Eval.CRegion`): `RegionsCollect` the collector, `RegionsCensus` the census, which frees nothing, or `RegionsOff` neither. `withRegions` runs an arena's setup and teardown with them running, `regionsRunning` reads what they run for, and `inRegion` runs a force in a region of its own.
