- **The C data layer frees the attribute sets, list headers, lambdas, builtins' partial applications and strings with context that a force of a thunk allocated, once the force returns and nothing reaches them.** The layer freed nothing before its session ended, so one evaluation over nixpkgs held every set it had ever made, and sets are more than half of what it allocates. At the end of each force that allocated at least 16 KiB, a collector marks from the stores the force made into older cells and with-scopes and the roots it registered, entering only what no such close inside it already settled, and marks the force's whole region again once the closes inside have tenured 1 MiB since its last such mark; what the mark leaves unreached is freed. Thunk cells, environments and strings stay until the session ends. An evaluation under `NOVA_NIX_C_STATS` or `NOVA_NIX_REGION_STATS` frees nothing, since those censuses count what the evaluation allocated. A force at the top of an evaluation that allocates less than 16 KiB marks nothing, so what it leaves stays until the session ends. (#466)
- **Library API:** `SessionSettings` takes a fifth field, `sessionRegions`, what the session runs regions for (`Nix.Eval.CRegion`): `RegionsCollect` the collector, `RegionsCensus` the census, which frees nothing, or `RegionsOff` neither. `withRegions` runs an arena's setup and teardown with them running, `regionsRunning` reads what they run for, and `inRegion` runs a force in a region of its own.
