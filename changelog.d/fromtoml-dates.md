- **`builtins.fromTOML` refuses a date or a time with upstream's `while parsing TOML: Dates and times are not supported`, where it returned the text as a string.** Upstream (fromTOML.cc, the same at 2.24.9, 2.34.9 and 2.36.0) turns an offset or local date-time, a local date or a local time into a value only under the `parse-toml-timestamps` experimental feature, and otherwise refuses it once the document has parsed. nova-nix has no experimental features, so it refuses each one, after the rest of the document has parsed, as upstream does by default.
