- **`nova-nix eval` writes a census of regions to the file `NOVA_NIX_REGION_STATS` names: at the end of each force of a thunk, the bytes the force allocated and the bytes of them still reachable, and the most bytes collectors freeing at those points would have held.** The C data layer frees nothing before its session ends, and the census `NOVA_NIX_C_STATS` writes says what is unreachable once evaluation returns, not when it became so. A collector freeing at the end of a force can free only what the force allocated and nothing reaches through the older cells and with-scopes it wrote into or the roots it registered. The census records every such write, marks from them at each force's end, stopping at older objects, and frees nothing. It reports the closes by the bytes their regions allocated, the bytes allocated outside every force, which no such collector frees, and, for collectors that mark only at the regions past a threshold of bytes, the closes that mark, their work and the most bytes held at once, freeing single objects or only whole blocks at the allocators' block sizes and at four smaller ones. It also counts what a close reached after an earlier close found it unreachable, the pointer a collector would have freed under. And it simulates collectors that tenure what their closes mark: past their threshold each close marks only what no such close of theirs inside it tenured, and a close marks its region whole when it is outermost or, for a collector with a budget of 1, 16 or 256 MiB, when the closes inside have tenured at least that since; their roots are the stores into tenured or older cells, which a mark of the whole reads again for each region they are older than, and the pointers their marks find into untenured objects of regions around. It reports each one's work, the bytes its marks of the whole free and the most bytes it holds beside the collector that marks again at every close, and checks that none frees anything a mark reaches. `profiling/bench.sh --memory` runs it over its slice and reports it. Without the variable an allocation tests one more flag. (#466)
- **Library API:** `SessionSettings` takes a fifth field, `sessionRegionCensus`, whether the session runs the census (`Nix.Eval.CRegion`); `RegionCensusOff` runs none.
