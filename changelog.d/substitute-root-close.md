- **A substitution reads a cache's NAR only to the archive's end, as upstream does, so a NAR followed by bytes the archive does not hold substitutes as the archive, held to the narinfo's NarHash and NarSize over the archive's bytes alone.** Upstream substitutes a path by importing the NAR it reads from the cache through `restorePath` (`LocalStore::addToStore` in local-store.cc at 2.36.0), whose `parseDump` stops at the root node's close and never reads what follows, and it checks the NarHash and NarSize against the bytes read to there. nova-nix parsed the download as an archive that must end with it, so such a NAR failed as malformed and the attempt was retried.
