- **A built output's registration names the `.drv` that built it, so `nova-nix push` writes its narinfo with upstream's `Deriver` field.** Upstream records the derivation as the deriver of every output it registers from a build (`newInfo.deriver = drvPath`, local-derivation-goal.cc:2738 at 2.24.9), and a narinfo names it by its basename. nova-nix registered a built output with no deriver, so its pushed narinfo had no `Deriver` line, while a substituted path kept the deriver its cache's narinfo named. Upstream's `keep-derivations` and `keep-outputs`, through which its garbage collector reads the deriver, are still not implemented.
- **Library API:** `Nix.Push` exports `preparePath`, the NAR and the narinfo a push uploads for one store path.
