- **A substituter listed twice, in `substituters`, `extra-substituters` or again with `--substituter`, is asked once, at its first listing, as upstream drops a substituter it already has.** Every listing was kept, so a cache listed twice was asked again for a path whose NAR it had failed to deliver or no longer held, and a narinfo it served that was passed over was reported twice. Two listings are one when they are spelled alike. Upstream compares them once parsed, so spellings that differ only in percent-escapes, the order of a query's parameters, a fragment or a port's leading zeros are one substituter there and two here, while a spelling with a trailing slash and one without are two in both. The executable's `containers` floor rises to 0.6.0.1, the first with `nubOrd`.
