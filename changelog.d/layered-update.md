- **`//` with a right operand of at most 16 attributes makes a layer on the left operand holding the right operand's attributes alone, as upstream 2.34 does, where it copied both operands into a new set.** Sets made at `//` held most of the bytes of sets nova-nix allocates over a nixpkgs walk (#628), and upstream 2.34 layers such a right operand on the left while the left's chain of layers holds fewer than eight sets, copying the right operand alone (`Bindings::maxLayers`, `eval-attrset-update-layer-rhs-threshold` and `ExprOpUpdate::eval`, in attr-set.hh, eval-settings.hh and eval.cc). A lookup searches the layers top first, and a walk over the set reads each name once, in symbol order, from its topmost layer, so every read answers as the merged set did. A larger right operand, or a left whose chain holds eight sets already, is merged into a new set as before. The census counts the entries a layer copies, the right operand's, as upstream adds them to `nrOpUpdateValuesCopied`, and counts a layer's own bytes, and its own entries among the sizes of the sets it reaches; the census, the object graph and the region collector follow a layer to the set under it. Over the first 2,000 attributes of the memory suite's walk of nixpkgs 26.05, `//` copies 24.5 million entries where it copied 50.1 million, the sets it makes take 433.9 MiB where they took 829.9 MiB, and in runs on macOS peak RSS falls by 287 to 336 MiB.
