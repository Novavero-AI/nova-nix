- **An environment of one to seven values and no scopes takes 8 bytes and 8 per value, as upstream's does: a call's takes 16 bytes where it took 24, and a `let`'s, a `rec` set's or a call's with formals of that size 8 bytes less than it did.** Over the first 2,000 attributes of nixpkgs 24.11 environments took 674.5 MiB, 2.2 times upstream 2.24.9's 304.6 MiB (#529), and 13.8 million of their 24.8 million were calls' of one value, each a header word, its value and its parent. Upstream's `Env` is a parent and its values (eval.hh:135-139 at 2.24.9). Such an environment's first word is now its parent's address, with its value count in the three low bits that every environment's 8-byte alignment leaves clear, so everything it points to is still found from the environment alone; an environment of more values, or with a lazy scope or with-scopes, keeps its header word. On `profiling/stress.nix` environments take 15.2 MB where they took 18.5 MB, and wall time on it and on `fib 30` is unchanged within run-to-run noise. What any program evaluates to is unchanged.
