- **`NIX_PATH` keeps a `channel:` or `flake:` entry whole and splits an unlisted `scheme://` entry, as upstream's parser does.** A colon stayed inside its entry whenever `//` followed it, so `channel:nixos-24.11` broke into `channel` and `nixos-24.11` while `foo://x` was kept as one entry. Upstream's `EvalSettings::parseNixPath` (`src/libexpr/eval-settings.cc` at Nix 2.24.9) decides at an entry's first colon from the text after the entry's last `=`: `channel` and `flake` keep the entry whole whatever follows, `http`, `https`, `file`, `channel`, `git`, `s3` and `ssh` keep it whole when `//` follows, and the entry then extends through exactly one more colon-free run, so `https://example.com:8080/x` still splits after the host and `channel:channel:x` is `channel:channel` then `x`. The split now follows that rule, matched entry by entry against `builtins.nixPath` from `nix-instantiate` 2.33.2, whose parser is unchanged from 2.24.9. An empty entry is dropped, as upstream's `builtins.nixPath` drops it; it was not inert here, since `<name>` resolved against the filesystem root through it (`NIX_PATH=a::b` made `<etc>` resolve to `/etc`). An entry containing a space is kept whole where upstream's settings round trip would split it, since a Windows path may contain one. The Windows drive rule stays: `C:\x` and `nixpkgs=C:/x` are one entry each, where upstream, which has no drive rule, splits a drive path at its letter. Neither a URL nor a channel entry is fetched yet, so for those entries this changes what `builtins.nixPath` reports, not what `<nixpkgs>` resolves to.
