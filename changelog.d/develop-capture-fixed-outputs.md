- **`Nix.Develop.Capture.captureDerivation` captures a derivation with a fixed output beside others, or under another name than `out`, input-addressed, as upstream's `nix develop` does, and refuses only a floating content-addressed or impure output, the one reason its refusal gives.** Upstream's `getDerivationEnvironment` (develop.cc at 2.36.0) defers every input-addressed and fixed output before anything reads the outputs together, and its reading of a `.drv` (derivation/aterm.cc) checks no output against another, so such a derivation is captured; nova-nix refused it with `the derivation has a floating content-addressed or impure output`, which it has not. nova-nix's evaluator makes no such derivation, so `develop` and `print-dev-env` are unchanged.
