- **`builtins.toJSON` escapes backspace and form feed as `\b` and `\f`, as upstream does.** It wrote them as `\u0008` and `\u000c`. Upstream serializes through nlohmann's `dump()`, whose escaper has two-character escapes for the quote, the backslash, backspace, form feed, newline, carriage return and tab, and `\u00xx` only for the other control characters. The same rendering now writes a structured derivation's `__json`, whose bytes are hashed into its store path. (#36)
