- **`--help` and `--version` are answered after every command, as they were after `build` and `trust`, and a run whose stdout has lost its reader, as `nova-nix --help | head -1`, reports nothing and ends with the status its work gives it.** `eval --help`, `push --help`, `store --help`, `store delete --help` and `store gc --help` failed with the parser's `unknown ... flag: --help` and status 1. A write to stdout after its reader had gone, such as the help's second line once `head -1` had read the first, failed with `error: <stdout>: hFlush: resource vanished (Broken pipe)` and status 1, and `store delete`, which prints each path as it deletes it, stopped after the first. Upstream's main thread blocks SIGPIPE (`startSignalHandlerThread`, libutil/unix/signals.cc at 2.24.9), so a write through `std::cout` to a pipe with no reader fails with EPIPE, which only sets the stream's error state: nix-instantiate writes its result that way (`processExpr` in nix-instantiate.cc), nothing is reported, and the command goes on to its own exit status. nova-nix now drops the rest of a write to stdout once its reader has gone, and still reports any other failure of a write.
