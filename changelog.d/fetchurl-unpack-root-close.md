- **`builtin:fetchurl` with `unpack` reads a NAR body only to the archive's end, as upstream does, so bytes a server sends after the archive are left unread rather than failing the fetch.** Upstream's builtin restores the download through `parseDump` (fetchurl.cc and archive.cc at 2.36.0), which stops at the root node's close and never reads what follows, so the output is the archive and its hash the archive's. nova-nix parsed the body as an archive that must end with it, and failed a body with any byte after the root node as a malformed NAR.
- **Library API:** `Nix.Store.sinkNarStream` takes an `ArchiveEnd` first: `AtEndOfInput` for the archive that ends its input, as it always read one, and `AtRootClose` for one that stops at its root node's close, leaving the rest of the input unread. Either way the digest and size are of the bytes the parser consumed.
