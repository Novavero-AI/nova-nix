- **A variable is looked up only as its kind requires, as upstream's `EvalState::lookupVar` does, and a closure trimmed under a `with` shares the scopes it sees.** A variable a `let`, a recursive set or a global binds was also searched for in the `with` scopes when no name table held it, and a `with` variable no scope held was searched for again in every name table. Neither second search could find anything, since the parser decides each variable's kind before evaluation, and a closure trimmed to its captures under a `with` held a copy of its scopes with the builtins appended for one of them. It now shares the scopes of the environment it is made in, and one made outside every `with` holds none.
- **Library API:** `Nix.Eval.CEnv` removes `cenvRootScope` and `cenvAllocWithScopes`, which built a trimmed closure's copy of its scopes.
