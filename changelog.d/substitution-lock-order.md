- **A substitution takes its output locks in the same order as a build, so the two cannot deadlock.** A multi-output substitution locked its outputs in the derivation's order and held each until the batch registered, while a build locks them in store path order. Two processes working on the same derivation could each hold the lock the other was waiting for, and the blocking lock has no timeout, so both hung. Both now take their locks through one ordering.
