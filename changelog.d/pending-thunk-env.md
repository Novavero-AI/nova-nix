- **Evaluation spends a fraction of the time it did collecting garbage: the drvPaths of firefox, git, python3 and gimp from nixpkgs evaluate in 6.7 seconds where they took 24.5, and a minimal NixOS system's in 19 seconds where it took 149.** A thunk not yet forced reached the environment its code runs in through a GHC stable pointer, freed only when the thunk was forced, and nixpkgs leaves most of the thunks it makes unforced: those two evaluations ended holding about 1.9 million and 3.9 million such pointers. GHC marks every stable pointer on every collection, minor ones included, so collecting took 74% and 87% of the time. A pending thunk now holds its environment's C pointer, as upstream's holds its `Env *`, and collecting takes 9% and 12%. A frame whose bindings refer to it, those of a `let`, a `rec` set or a function's formals, is allocated before the thunks that point at it and its slots are filled after, as upstream builds `env2` in `ExprLet::eval`, `ExprAttrs::eval` and `EvalState::callFunction` (eval.cc at 2.24.9). The peak memory footprint falls by 8% on both evaluations, measured back to back on one machine, and the drvPaths are unchanged.
- **Library API:** `Nix.Eval.Types` drops `mkSyntheticThunk`, whose callers use `mkThunkBc`, and `allocCSlots` and `fillCSlots`, which `tieFrame` replaces: it builds a frame whose slots hold thunks that point back at it. A pending thunk's cell holds its env, so `mkThunkBc` and `mkThunk`, and `cheapThunkBc` and `cheapThunk` where they fall back to them, force the `Env` when the `Thunk` value is demanded, which is when its cell is allocated (as it is stored in a slot, a list or a set), not when the evaluator forces the thunk. A knot built over these constructors, an env made from slots holding thunks that point back at it, therefore loops while its slots are built; build it with `tieFrame` instead. `cthunkNewBc` in `Nix.Eval.CThunk` takes the pending payload as an `nn_env_t *`: both evaluators' force paths read it back through `pendingThunkEnv`, and arena teardown no longer frees it, so a caller passing a StablePtr there leaks it and crashes when the thunk is forced. `cthunkPayload` answers that env pointer for a pending thunk, and `pendingThunkEnv` answers it as an `Env`. `Nix.Eval.CThunk` drops `cthunkSetPayload`.
