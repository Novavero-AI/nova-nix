- **`builtin:fetchurl` retries a transient failure with backoff before moving to the next URL, as upstream's transfer layer does.** One timeout, reset or 5xx against a flapping host used to end that URL's attempt at once, so a short outage on every listed mirror cost the whole fetch. Each URL now gets upstream's five attempts (the `download-attempts` default in `libstore/filetransfer.hh` at 2.24.9), waiting 250 ms before the first retry and doubling after that, with upstream's jitter on the exponent, and each retry is announced on stderr with upstream's `; retrying in N ms` suffix. Upstream's classification decides what a retry can outlive (`libstore/filetransfer.cc` at 2.24.9): a 404 or 410, a 401, 403 or 407, any other 4xx except 408 and 429, a 501, 505 or 511, a malformed or unsupported URL, a redirect loop, a failure writing the output, and a download that verifies wrong are deterministic and move straight to the next URL; everything else, name resolution and TLS included, is retried. A retry restarts the download from the first byte with a fresh hash, where upstream resumes from the written offset only when the server accepts ranges. The policy lives in `BuildConfig` as `bcFetchRetry`, a `FetchRetryPolicy` whose default is upstream's, and the retry loop, delay formula and both classifications are exported from `Nix.Http` with their effects injected, so the policy is tested without a clock or a network.
