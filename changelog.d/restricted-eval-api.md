- **`Nix.Eval.Types.MonadEval` has three new methods with no defaults,** `evalPolicy`, `checkUri` and `resolveSymlinks`, and `doesPathExist` takes a `PathExistence` first argument (`ExistsAsEntry` or `ExistsAsDirectory`, the two questions `builtins.pathExists` asks); every instance defined outside this package needs them. The new module `Nix.Eval.Policy` holds the policy record (`EvalPolicy`, `unrestrictedPolicy`) and the pure decisions behind the modes (`isAllowedPath`, `isAllowedUri`, `uriAccess` and their messages), and `Nix.Eval.Types` re-exports the record. `Nix.Builtins.builtinEnv` and `builtinEnvWithScope` take an `EvalPolicy` first argument, since the impure constants exist by policy; `Nix.Builtins.searchPathRoots` reads the roots back from a search path, and `isNixPathPseudoUrl` says which entries name a download. `Nix.Eval.IO.EvalState` gains `esPolicy` and `esAllowedPaths`, with `allowEvalPath` to seed the allowed roots. `Nix.Eval` exports `FetchGitArgs (..)` and `gitInputUrl`, the URL form a `fetchGit` input is checked and reported in. `Nix.Config.NixConfig` gains `ncAllowedUris`, `ncRestrictEval` and `ncPureEval`.
