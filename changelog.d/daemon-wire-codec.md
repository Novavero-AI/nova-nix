- **Library API:** the new modules `Nix.Daemon.Wire` and `Nix.Daemon.Protocol` read and write the worker protocol upstream's `nix-daemon` speaks, as upstream 2.35.4 reads and writes it at every protocol version its serialisers distinguish. `Nix.Daemon.Wire` is the framing: 64-bit little-endian numbers, each refused above the largest value of the type upstream reads it into; strings padded with zeros to a multiple of eight, refused when the padding is not zeros; lists; and framed streams, read a chunk at a time. Its `Decoder` reads incrementally (`startDecoding`, `feedInput`), so a connection can hand it each read as it arrives. `Nix.Daemon.Protocol` holds the values the protocol carries and their serialisers: versions and features, the handshake's words and what a daemon tells its client after it, store paths, a path's info with its NAR hash, signatures and content address, derived paths, realisations, build results, a collection's action and paths, and the messages and errors a daemon sends while it works. Each reader refuses what upstream refuses, and each writer refuses what upstream's does, in upstream's words, but for a malformed hash, which is worded as `Nix.Hash` words it, after 2.24.9. Realisations and a build's outputs follow 2.35's `realisation-with-path-not-hash` feature where both sides list it, and the older JSON form of a build's outputs where they do not, as 2.35.4 does. `Nix.Hash` exports `parseAnyHash`, `parseHashAlgo`, `digestByLength`, `sriDigest` and `unknownHashAlgo`, which `Nix.Eval` held privately, and the library depends on `binary`.
