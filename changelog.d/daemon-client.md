- **Library API:** the new `Nix.Daemon.Client` is a client of a daemon that speaks upstream's worker protocol, as upstream's remote store is one, over a transport it is given (`Nix.Daemon.Connection.Transport`). `runClient` makes upstream's client handshake, offering the newest version and the features the codec carries (`offeredVersion`) and refusing a daemon older than 1.18, then runs a session's operations (`ClientT`): `setOptions`, the queries (`isValidPath`, `queryValidPaths`, `querySubstitutablePaths`, `queryReferrers`, `queryValidDerivers`, `queryDerivationOutputMap`, `queryAllValidPaths`, `queryPathInfo`, `queryMissing`), the framed uploads `addToStore` and `addToStoreNar`, `buildPaths`, `buildPathsWithResults`, `ensurePath`, `addTempRoot` and `addIndirectRoot`. What the daemon says while it works goes to a report, which `loggerReport` gives a `Nix.Logger.Logger` as upstream's simple logger reports it. A daemon's error is a `DaemonFailed` that `attempt` catches, the session going on; a request the agreed version does not carry is not sent (`NotSent`); a broken connection is a `ConnectionLost`. `putRequest` writes a request as the daemon's `Nix.Daemon.Ops.getRequest` reads it back, and `putClientSettings` a client's settings. `parseStoreTarget` reads what `--store` names, upstream's `auto`, `daemon`, `unix://PATH` and `local` or a store directory, and `chooseStore` decides where a command's store operations go: by default through the daemon whenever its socket accepts a connection, root included, and to the store directly otherwise. No command uses the client yet.
