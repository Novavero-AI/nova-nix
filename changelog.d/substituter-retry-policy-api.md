- **`Nix.Substituter.httpStatusFailure` is gone, and the transfer layer's retry policy and failure classification live in `Nix.Http`.** `fetchStatusFailure`, which takes the URL and the response's `Status`, replaces it. `Nix.Http` exports `AttemptFailure (..)`, `attemptFailureMessage` and `catchSync`, which `Nix.Substituter` still re-exports; the retry policy (`FetchRetryPolicy (..)`, `defaultFetchRetryPolicy`, `RetryEffects (..)`, `ioRetryEffects`, `retryTransient`, `retryDelayMs`); and the classification (`TransferError (..)` and `statusError`, upstream's verdict on a status, `withTransfer` and `transferBodyReader`, which raise every failure of the connection as an `HttpException`, `transferFailureHandlers`, `fetchStatusFailure`, `fetchExceptionFailure`, which now takes an `HttpException` rather than any exception, and `fetchWriteFailure`, for an `IOException` writing a download). `Nix.Substituter.trySubstituteWith` substitutes under a given policy and effects; `trySubstitute` is it at upstream's default, and a build passes its `bcFetchRetry`.
