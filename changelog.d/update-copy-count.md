- **The census `NOVA_NIX_C_STATS` writes counts the entries `//` copies into the sets it makes, as upstream counts them in `nrOpUpdateValuesCopied`.** Over the first 5,000 attributes of nixpkgs 26.05, sets made at `//` hold 1,786.5 of the 2,378.3 MiB of sets nova-nix allocates, and upstream 2.34 copies fewer entries there than 2.24 does, by layering a small right operand on the left (`ExprOpUpdate::eval` in eval.cc), so the count shows how far nova-nix's `//` is from it. A `//` of two sets that hold entries counts the entries of the set it makes, as upstream adds the merged set's size (`ExprOpUpdate::eval` in eval.cc), and one with an empty operand, which gives the other operand and makes no set, counts none, as upstream's does. `profiling/bench.sh --memory` reports the count beside upstream's.
