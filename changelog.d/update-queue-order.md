- **A chain of `//` evaluates its operands last to first, as upstream 2.34 and later do, where it evaluated them first to last as 2.24.9 does: of two operands that fail, the later one fails the chain, `1 // "x"` refuses the string, and an operand's traces print after those of the operands after it.** Upstream 2.34's update queue gathers a chain by evaluating each update's right operand and then its left, checking each is a set as it is evaluated (`evalForUpdate`, eval.cc:2014-2019 at 2.34.9, the same at 2.35.4). Where the pinned releases differ, nova-nix follows the newest, and the parity rows whose chains hold two operands that fail run from 2.34.0.
