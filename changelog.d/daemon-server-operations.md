- **Library API:** the new modules `Nix.Daemon.Ops` and `Nix.Daemon.Connection` are a daemon's side of upstream's worker protocol, over a store and a transport they are given. `Nix.Daemon.Connection.serveConnection` runs one connection: upstream's handshake, from 1.18, then each operation the client asks, until its input ends. `Nix.Daemon.Ops` holds what each operation reads before its work begins (`getRequest`), what a client the daemon does not trust may not ask (`authorize`), what a client's settings change for its connection (`applyClientSettings`), and the store the operations run over (`DaemonStore`), a record of upstream's store calls in the protocol's own terms. The operations are those upstream's clients send to build, substitute, copy, query and collect through a daemon; another code is refused as upstream refuses one it does not know, and AddToStore before protocol 1.25 and AddToStoreNar before 1.23, whose uploads are not framed, are refused as their requests begin. A failure before an operation's work closes the connection and one during it is sent and the connection goes on, where upstream's daemon does each; what the store reports as it works reaches the client as upstream's daemon forwards its logger's calls; and an upload's frames are read past after its store stops, as upstream drains them, so the next operation starts at its first byte. Upstream's messages are kept without the colours its formatter adds. `Nix.Daemon.Ops` also writes each request and a client's settings as upstream's client writes them (`putRequest`, `putClientSettings`, `putUploadPathInfo`), refusing in upstream's words a request the agreed version cannot carry, and names the versions the requests change at. `Nix.Daemon.Protocol` reads and writes the replies the operations give (`Missing`, `GCResult`, a derivation's outputs, the roots, build results with the paths they answer) and names the build and substitution activities and the two log-line results. `Nix.Daemon.Wire.DecodeFailed` carries the input after the bytes a refused read took, and `Nix.Daemon.Protocol` exports `parseContentAddressMethod`, `featureDeleteDeadSpecificReferrers` and `defaultExitStatus`.
