- **The library exports what its callers use, and an evaluation session alone sets up and tears down the C data layer.** The binding modules exported the layer's lifecycle, so a library caller could destroy the memory an open session was using, and exported names nothing outside their module used, which a caller could come to depend on. The lifecycle's foreign imports are in the internal `Nix.Eval.Arena`, where an evaluation session (`Nix.Eval.IO.withEvalSession`) calls them.
- **Library API:** the C data layer's lifecycle is internal to the library, as an evaluation session owns it (`Nix.Eval.IO.withEvalSession`): `Nix.Eval.Symbol` no longer exports `symbolInit` and `symbolDestroy`, `Nix.Eval.CThunk` `cthunkInit` and `cthunkDestroy`, `Nix.Eval.CEnv` `cenvInit` and `cenvDestroy`, `Nix.Eval.CBytecode` `cbcInit` and `cbcDestroy`, and `Nix.Eval.CCtxStr`, `Nix.Eval.CLambda` and `Nix.Eval.CList` `cctxstrFreeAll`, `clambdaFreeAll` and `clistFreeAll`, any of which, called inside a session, freed the session's memory under it. Names nothing outside their module uses are no longer exported: from `Nix.Eval.Types`, `marshalStringContext`, `unmarshalStringContext`, `unmarshalLambdaValue`, `withScopesForCapture` and `mkThunk`; from `Nix.Eval`, `BuiltinDef` and `ArgMode`; from `Nix.Eval.IO`, `EvalErrorKind`, `NixEvalError` and `NixAbortError`, which `runEvalIO` and `runEvalIOTraced` catch before a caller can meet them; from `Nix.Eval.Policy`, `modeInformation` and `forbiddenUriMessage`; from `Nix.Eval.CallDepth`, `maxCallDepthExceeded`; and from `Nix.Eval.CList`, `clistNew`, which made a list whose cells nothing exported could fill, `clistCount`, `clistGet`, `clistSet` and `clistToThunkList`: a list is built with `clistFromThunks` or `thunkListToCList` and read with `clistLen`, `clistIndex` and `clistThunks`. `Nix.Eval.Types.thunkToCPtr`, which was `unThunk`, is gone, and so are `Nix.Eval.CThunk.cthunkCount` and `cthunkGet`, with the count of thunks the arena kept for them.
