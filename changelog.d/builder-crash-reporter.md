The job each unsandboxed builder runs in now has `JOB_OBJECT_LIMIT_DIE_ON_UNHANDLED_EXCEPTION`, as the sandbox helper's job has, the counterpart of upstream having a crashing builder dump no core. The limit is the job's, so it holds for a process that sets its own error mode, as busybox-w32 does. The `process` floor rises to 1.6.8, the first release whose process handle holds the process and its job alone, the job the limit is set through.
