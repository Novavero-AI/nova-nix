- **A substituted NAR whose hash or size is not its narinfo's is refused as upstream refuses it, the hash checked before the size and each in upstream's words: `hash mismatch importing path '<path>';` with the specified and got hashes in SRI form on the lines after, or `size mismatch importing path '<path>';` with the two sizes.** nova-nix checked the size first and worded both its own way, as `NAR size mismatch: narinfo declares <n> bytes but the stream carried <m>` and `NAR hash mismatch: narinfo declares <hash> but downloaded bytes hash to <hash>`. Upstream's `LocalStore::addToStore` (local-store.cc at 2.36.0) checks the hash of the bytes it restored, then their count, and words both so. The streamed and the whole-NAR substitution and the daemon's import of a NAR now share the one check.
