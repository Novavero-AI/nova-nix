- **An interrupted command reports `error: interrupted by the user` and exits 1.** A SIGINT reached the CLI's main thread as GHC's `UserInterrupt`, which, uncaught, ended the process by the signal (exit 130) with nothing printed. Upstream's `checkInterrupt` throws `Interrupted("interrupted by the user")`, which `handleExceptions` (libmain/shared.cc at 2.24.9) logs as an error and exits 1 with; the CLI now does the same, and every other asynchronous exception still propagates.
