- **A hash's base64 is read as upstream reads it, up to the first `=`, so an SRI or base64 hash with text after its padding, such as `sha256-HS/n40Ng8S5N14DtEH/upwlxdzwCoOEJA40EMdCcLw4=io`, is the digest spelled before the padding.** The decoder removed every `=`, newline and carriage return and decoded what was left, so a character after the padding became part of the digest, giving a 33rd byte or an encoding it refused, and a last character whose unused bits were not zero was refused. The hashes nixpkgs gives `nmgui` on unstable, and `cosmic-workspaces-epoch`, `guake`, `journalist` and `quintom-cursor-theme` on 24.11, failed this way. Upstream's `base64Decode` (util.cc at 2.24.9, `base64::decode` in base-n.cc at 2.36.0) stops at the first `=` and reads nothing after it, skips a newline, refuses any other character outside the standard alphabet, a carriage return among them, and drops the bits left after the last whole byte whatever their value, and so does the decoder here, for `builtins.convertHash`, a fixed-output derivation's `outputHash`, and the `sha256` of `builtins.fetchurl`, `builtins.fetchTarball` and `builtins.path`. A hash's algorithm tag is split off as upstream's `getParsedTypeAndSRI` splits it (hash.cc at 2.24.9, `parseAnyHelper` at 2.36.0): at the first `:`, else at the first `-`, and a tag that names no known algorithm is an error, so a separator after the padding still decides the tag, and `sha256-...=:x` names the unknown algorithm `sha256-...=`. A hash not in SRI form is read as base16, nix32 or base64 by its length in bytes, as upstream counts it, so a character after the padding that UTF-8 spells in more than one byte makes the length wrong. A base64 digest that is refused or decodes to the wrong length fails with upstream's message for both, `invalid SRI hash '...'` or `invalid base-64 hash '...'`, since upstream catches the decoder's exception without rethrowing it and lets the length check report it (the `Hash` constructor in hash.cc at 2.24.9, `parseLowLevel` at 2.36.0).
- **Library API:** `Nix.Hash` exports the decoder, `base64ToBytes :: Text -> Maybe ByteString`.
