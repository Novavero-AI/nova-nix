- **A collection, a checked delete and `store gc --print-roots` read, for a store in a directory upstream's local store uses, the roots under upstream's `gcroots` and `profiles` in its state directory, `/nix/var/nix` for `/nix/store`, beside the store's own.** Upstream's clients keep their profiles and their out-links' roots there, and upstream's collector reads both directories, so a nova-nix store that serves them would otherwise collect what they keep.
- **Library API:** `Nix.Store.Import` imports a path from its NAR with what it claims of itself (`importNar`), refusing, in upstream's words, a path that must be signed and is neither signed by a key given nor content-addressed, a NAR whose hash or size is not the claim's, a tree that does not reproduce the stated content address, an address that does not make the path, and a derivation whose outputs are not the paths its inputs make (`checkDerivationOutputs`); and adds a text as a client adds a `.drv` (`addTextToStore`). The NAR arrives from a chunk source that ends where the archive ends. `collectGarbage` takes extra roots, kept alive as upstream's temporary roots are, and `deleteSpecificPaths` deletes named paths as upstream's collector deletes them: each with the paths that reference it, all named, referrers first (`referrersFirst`), stopping at the first that stays alive with upstream's refusal. `Nix.Substituter` exports `checkContentAddress`, `Nix.Store.Upstream` exports `upstreamStateDir`, and `Nix.Derivation` exports `fixedOutputModulo` and `inputAddressedModulo`, the modulo-hash rules the evaluator and the store share.
