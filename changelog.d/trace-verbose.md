- **`builtins.traceVerbose` returns its second argument without forcing or printing its first, unless `trace-verbose` is on, through `--trace-verbose` on `eval` and `build` or `trace-verbose` in nix.conf and NIX_CONFIG.** It was `builtins.trace` under another name, so every call forced its first argument and printed it to stderr, and `builtins.traceVerbose (throw "a") 1` failed where upstream gives `1`. Upstream binds the name to `prim_second` by default and to `prim_trace` under the setting (`primops.cc` at 2.24.9), and both are matched: without the setting `traceVerbose` never forces its first argument, applied directly, through a partial application or by a higher-order builtin, and with it `traceVerbose` forces and prints its first argument as `builtins.trace` does before it forces its second. Like `--restrict-eval` and `--pure-eval`, the flag only turns the setting on; upstream also derives a `--no-trace-verbose` from the setting, which nova-nix does not have.
- **Library API:** `Nix.Eval.Policy.EvalPolicy` gains `epTraceVerbose` and `Nix.Config.NixConfig` gains `ncTraceVerbose`, both off in `unrestrictedPolicy` and `defaultNixConfig`, as upstream's default is.
