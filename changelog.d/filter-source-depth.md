- **A filtered `builtins.path` or `builtins.filterSource` refuses an entry 64 levels below the source's root with `path '<path>' exceeds maximum NAR directory depth of 64`, as upstream's dump refuses it.** Upstream archives a filtered tree through the same dump as any other (`SourceAccessor::dumpPath` in archive.cc at 2.36.0), which refuses a node at depth 64, the root at 0, once the filter has kept it and before reading it. nova-nix walks a filtered tree itself to call the filter, and that walk counted no depth, so such a tree was archived and then refused only by the NAR parser, in its words rather than the dump's.
