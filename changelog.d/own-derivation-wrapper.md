The parity job probes it against upstream's `nix-instantiate` over a corpus of arguments in `test/data/derivation-probes.txt`, with the rows where the evaluator is known to differ from upstream listed beside it, and the test suite pins what the probes read. `NOTICE` names `pkgs/windows/mirrors.nix`, copied from nixpkgs, with its license. The new wrapper's identifiers are interned at startup in place of upstream's, as upstream interns its own wrapper's, so an error that names attributes in interning order can name another first: a call with the unexpected arguments `zzz` and `x`, written in that order in a file, named `x` and now names `zzz`.
