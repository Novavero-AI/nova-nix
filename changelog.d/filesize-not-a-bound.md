- **A substitute whose NAR file is larger than its narinfo's FileSize is downloaded, as upstream downloads it, where the substituter stopped with `NAR body exceeds its download ceiling`.** The download cap was the smaller of a ceiling derived from the signed NarSize and the narinfo's FileSize, which no signature covers. cache.nixos.org names its zstd NARs by their NarHash, so every store path with the same NAR shares one file, and an upload that compresses that NAR again replaces the file whose FileSize and FileHash an earlier narinfo records. In the closure of the x86_64-linux stdenv of nixpkgs 26.05, `/nix/store/s52vdyj97g3adv6qb431v8m2kccs0bzz-linux-headers-6.18.7` declares a FileSize of 1891795 bytes, while the file its URL serves is 1891934 bytes and decompresses to the NAR its signed NarHash and NarSize name, so a build that substituted that stdenv failed. Upstream reads FileSize only for progress and download statistics (`build/substitution-goal.cc` and `misc.cc` at 2.35.4, `build/substitution-goal.cc` and `store-api.cc` at 2.24.9), never compares a download with FileHash, and checks a substituted NAR against NarHash and NarSize alone (`local-store.cc`). The cap is now the ceiling of the signed NarSize, which is NarSize plus the larger of 64 KiB and a 64th of NarSize, so a narinfo whose signature verifies still cannot buy a larger download, and a body past that ceiling still fails at once, without a retry. FileSize is still parsed and validated as before.
