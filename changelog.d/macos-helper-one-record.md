- **On macOS the sandbox helper reports at most one failure, so a build whose setup step fails names that step even when the helper also lost its watch on the build log, where it was refused as a status that cannot be read.** The builder's process reported a failed setup step on the helper's status channel itself, and the helper, after it reaped that process, wrote a second record when it could no longer watch the log; nova-nix refuses a status of two records as one it cannot read, losing the step that failed. The builder's process now reports on a pipe of its own, which its exec closes, and the helper relays what it says, reporting the lost log only when the builder's process reported nothing. Upstream's builder reports a failed step through the one pipe it reads the builder's setup from.
